01 / Flashcards

Know Your Terms

Tap any card to flip it and reveal the definition + example.

📋
Security Policy
[ TAP TO FLIP ]
POLICY
A formal, mandatory document that defines rules and obligations enforced by authority (management or regulation).
📌 Ex: "All employees MUST use MFA to access company systems."
📖
Security Guideline
[ TAP TO FLIP ]
GUIDELINE
Recommended, flexible best practices that provide direction — not mandatory, but highly advised.
📌 Ex: "It is recommended to use a password manager."
📐
Security Standard
[ TAP TO FLIP ]
STANDARD
Specific, measurable technical or procedural requirements that support policies — often industry-defined.
📌 Ex: "Passwords must be min. 12 chars with upper, lower, number & symbol."
🔐
Password Policy
[ TAP TO FLIP ]
POLICY
A mandatory organizational rule governing how passwords are created, used, and maintained to prevent unauthorized access.
📌 Ex: "Passwords must be changed every 90 days."
⚠️
Risk Assessment
[ TAP TO FLIP ]
GUIDELINE
A recommended process of identifying, analyzing, and evaluating cybersecurity risks to inform decision-making.
📌 Ex: NIST's Risk Management Framework guidance.
🚨
Incident Response
[ TAP TO FLIP ]
STANDARD
A defined, measurable procedure for detecting, containing, and recovering from cybersecurity incidents.
📌 Ex: ISO/IEC 27035 Incident Management Standard.

Incident Response
✅ STANDARD — Specific, measurable steps
Follows documented, measurable procedures (like ISO 27035) with defined phases: Identification → Containment → Eradication → Recovery.
📋
Acceptable Use Policy
✅ POLICY — Mandatory rule
Defines what employees are and aren't allowed to do with company systems. Enforced with disciplinary action.
🔒
Encryption Guideline
✅ GUIDELINE — Best practice recommendation
Recommends encryption methods (like AES-256) but leaves teams flexibility to implement based on their environment.
💳
PCI DSS
✅ STANDARD — Industry compliance benchmark
Payment Card Industry Data Security Standard — a specific, measurable set of requirements for protecting cardholder data.
04 / Framework Comparison

ISO vs NIST vs CIS

Click a framework to explore its purpose, complexity, and ideal use case.

ISO 27001
International Organization for Standardization
THE GLOBAL CERTIFICATION STANDARD
🎯
Purpose
Establish, implement, maintain, and continually improve an Information Security Management System (ISMS)
⚙️
Complexity
High — requires formal audits, documentation, and certification process
🏢
Best Use Case
Large enterprises, multinationals, companies needing internationally recognized certification
🌍
Scope
International — recognized globally across industries and governments
📄
Output
ISO 27001 Certification — audited and awarded by accredited bodies
💰
Cost/Effort
Very high — requires significant investment in time, auditors, and documentation
NIST CSF
National Institute of Standards and Technology — Cybersecurity Framework
THE FLEXIBLE RISK-BASED FRAMEWORK
🎯
Purpose
Provide a flexible, risk-based approach to managing cybersecurity risk across 5 core functions: Identify, Protect, Detect, Respond, Recover
⚙️
Complexity
Medium — scalable and adaptable to any organization size
🏢
Best Use Case
US federal agencies, critical infrastructure, any org wanting a voluntary framework
🌍
Scope
US-centric but globally adopted; especially strong in critical infrastructure sectors
📄
Output
Customized security profile and roadmap — no formal certification
💰
Cost/Effort
Moderate — free framework, cost depends on implementation scope
CIS Controls
Center for Internet Security — Critical Security Controls
THE ACTIONABLE QUICK-WIN CONTROLS
🎯
Purpose
Provide 18 prioritized, actionable security controls to defend against the most common cyber attacks
⚙️
Complexity
Low-Medium — prescriptive and easy to implement, especially for smaller teams
🏢
Best Use Case
SMBs, organizations new to cybersecurity, teams wanting fast, tangible improvements
🌍
Scope
Global, community-driven; implementation groups (IG1/IG2/IG3) scale by org size
📄
Output
Prioritized list of technical controls — CIS-CAT tool available for benchmarking
💰
Cost/Effort
Low — free controls documentation, quick wins achievable in days/weeks
