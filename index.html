01 / Flashcards

Know Your Terms

Tap any card to flip it and reveal the definition + example.

📋
Security Policy
[ TAP TO FLIP ]
POLICY
A formal, mandatory document that defines rules and obligations enforced by authority (management or regulation).
📌 Ex: "All employees MUST use MFA to access company systems."
📖
Security Guideline
[ TAP TO FLIP ]
GUIDELINE
Recommended, flexible best practices that provide direction — not mandatory, but highly advised.
📌 Ex: "It is recommended to use a password manager."
📐
Security Standard
[ TAP TO FLIP ]
STANDARD
Specific, measurable technical or procedural requirements that support policies — often industry-defined.
📌 Ex: "Passwords must be min. 12 chars with upper, lower, number & symbol."
🔐
Password Policy
[ TAP TO FLIP ]
POLICY
A mandatory organizational rule governing how passwords are created, used, and maintained to prevent unauthorized access.
📌 Ex: "Passwords must be changed every 90 days."
⚠️
Risk Assessment
[ TAP TO FLIP ]
GUIDELINE
A recommended process of identifying, analyzing, and evaluating cybersecurity risks to inform decision-making.
📌 Ex: NIST's Risk Management Framework guidance.
🚨
Incident Response
[ TAP TO FLIP ]
STANDARD
A defined, measurable procedure for detecting, containing, and recovering from cybersecurity incidents.
📌 Ex: ISO/IEC 27035 Incident Management Standard.
✅
Acceptable Use Policy
[ TAP TO FLIP ]
POLICY
A mandatory policy defining how employees may use company devices, internet access, email, and digital systems.
📌 Ex: "Installing unauthorized software on company laptops is prohibited."
🧠
Security Awareness Training
[ TAP TO FLIP ]
GUIDELINE
A recommended practice to regularly train users on phishing, social engineering, and safe behavior online.
📌 Ex: Quarterly phishing simulations with awareness refreshers.
💳
PCI DSS
[ TAP TO FLIP ]
STANDARD
An industry security standard with specific controls for organizations that store, process, or transmit cardholder data.
📌 Ex: Cardholder databases must be encrypted and access strictly controlled.
🔑
Access Control Standard
[ TAP TO FLIP ]
STANDARD
A measurable set of technical requirements that enforce least privilege and role-based access to systems.
📌 Ex: "Privileged accounts must use MFA and be reviewed every 30 days."

Password Policy
✅ POLICY — Mandatory enforcement
A formal, enforceable mandate that sets rules employees must follow. Organizations enforce consequences for non-compliance.
⚠️
Risk Assessment
✅ GUIDELINE — Recommended process
A recommended practice that provides direction on evaluating risks. Organizations adapt the process to their context.
🚨
Incident Response
✅ STANDARD — Specific, measurable steps
Follows documented, measurable procedures (like ISO 27035) with defined phases: Identification → Containment → Eradication → Recovery.
📋
Acceptable Use Policy
✅ POLICY — Mandatory rule
Defines what employees are and aren't allowed to do with company systems. Enforced with disciplinary action.
🔒
Encryption Guideline
✅ GUIDELINE — Best practice recommendation
Recommends encryption methods (like AES-256) but leaves teams flexibility to implement based on their environment.
💳
PCI DSS
✅ STANDARD — Industry compliance benchmark
Payment Card Industry Data Security Standard — a specific, measurable set of requirements for protecting cardholder data.
🗃️
Data Retention Policy
✅ POLICY — Mandatory rule
Defines how long data must be stored and when it must be securely deleted based on legal and business requirements.
🏠
Remote Work Guideline
✅ GUIDELINE — Recommended practice
Advises safe remote behavior such as using VPNs, locking screens, and avoiding public Wi-Fi for sensitive work.
🔑
Access Control Standard
✅ STANDARD — Specific, measurable requirements
Defines concrete controls for least privilege, role-based access, MFA, and periodic access reviews.

Guideline
Recommended best practices. Flexible, adaptable to context.
Standard
Specific, measurable technical requirements. Often industry-defined.
