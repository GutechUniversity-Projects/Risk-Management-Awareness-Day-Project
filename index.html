01 / Flashcards

Know Your Terms

Tap any card to flip it and reveal the definition + example.

📋
Security Policy
[ TAP TO FLIP ]
POLICY
A formal, mandatory document that defines rules and obligations enforced by authority.
📌 Ex: "All employees MUST use MFA to access company systems."
📖
Security Guideline
[ TAP TO FLIP ]
GUIDELINE
Recommended, flexible best practices that provide direction — not mandatory.
📌 Ex: "It is recommended to use a password manager."
📐
Security Standard
[ TAP TO FLIP ]
STANDARD
Specific, measurable technical or procedural requirements that support policies.
📌 Ex: "Passwords must be min. 12 chars with upper, lower, number & symbol."
🔐
Password Policy
[ TAP TO FLIP ]
POLICY
A mandatory organizational rule governing how passwords are created, used, and maintained.
📌 Ex: "Passwords must be changed every 90 days."
⚠️
Risk Assessment
[ TAP TO FLIP ]
GUIDELINE
A recommended process of identifying, analyzing, and evaluating cybersecurity risks.
📌 Ex: NIST's Risk Management Framework guidance.
🚨
Incident Response
[ TAP TO FLIP ]
STANDARD
A defined, measurable procedure for detecting, containing, and recovering from incidents.
📌 Ex: ISO/IEC 27035 Incident Management Standard.
✅
Acceptable Use Policy
[ TAP TO FLIP ]
POLICY
A mandatory policy defining how employees may use company devices and digital systems.
📌 Ex: "Installing unauthorized software on company laptops is prohibited."
🧠
Security Awareness Training
[ TAP TO FLIP ]
GUIDELINE
A recommended practice to regularly train users on phishing and safe behavior.
📌 Ex: Quarterly phishing simulations with awareness refreshers.
💳
PCI DSS
[ TAP TO FLIP ]
STANDARD
An industry security standard with specific controls for organizations handling cardholder data.
📌 Ex: Cardholder databases must be encrypted and access strictly controlled.
🔑
Access Control Standard
[ TAP TO FLIP ]
STANDARD
A measurable set of requirements that enforce least privilege and role-based access.
📌 Ex: "Privileged accounts must use MFA and be reviewed every 30 days."

📄 Cybersecurity Frameworks — Cheat Sheet

Quick reference guide · Policies, Guidelines & Standards

📋 The Three Pillars

Policy
Mandatory rules enforced by authority. Non-compliance = consequences.
Guideline
Recommended best practices. Flexible, adaptable to context.
Standard
Specific, measurable technical requirements. Often industry-defined.

🌍 ISO 27001

What it is

Who it's for

Key output

Complexity
